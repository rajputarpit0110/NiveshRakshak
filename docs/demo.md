# 1-Click Investor Protection Demo Flow (For Hackathon Judges)

## Overview
Judges can evaluate the end-to-end platform in **under 2 minutes** either by clicking the prominent **"⚡ 1-Click Investor Demo"** button on the navbar or navigating the live screens manually.

---

## The Signature 6-Stage Journey

### Scenario:
A retail investor notices an unexplained deduction of ₹2,500 in their Zerodha trading ledger.

1. **Step 1: Problem Detection (Confusion)**
   - Click **"⚡ 1-Click Investor Demo"** or open **Command Center**.
   - Observe the alert: ₹2,500 unexplained ledger debit detected.

2. **Step 2: Document Audit (Evidence)**
   - Navigate to **Document Audit**.
   - Notice the side-by-side ledger preview.
   - The AI isolates line item `14-03-2024: Sundry Admin Maintenance Fee - Debit: ₹2,500.00`.
   - Explains that undisclosed administrative charges violate SEBI transparency circulars.

3. **Step 3: Verified Statutory Grounding (Right Verification)**
   - Switch to **Rights Assistant**.
   - Inspect the grounded response citing:
     - *SEBI Circular on Transparency of Brokerage & Ledger Debits* (Section 1).
     - *SEBI Investor Charter for Stock Brokers* (Section 2.1).
   - Expand the `<SourceCitation />` cards to see real circular passages.

4. **Step 4: Evidence Completeness Check (Readiness)**
   - Navigate to **Grievance Studio**.
   - Notice the **Evidence Completeness Engine**:
     - ✓ Financial Ledger attached
     - ✓ Contract note attached
     - ⚠ Agreed Tariff Sheet missing
     - Completeness: **72%**
   - Action recommended: *"Upload the fee schedule to strengthen your grievance."*

5. **Step 5: Legal Complaint Drafting & Quality Scoring**
   - Click **"Analyze & Generate Draft"**.
   - Review the formal legal notice addressed to Zerodha Compliance.
   - Observe the **Complaint Quality Score**: **88/100 (EXCELLENT)**.
   - Click **"Download PDF"** to receive an official, branded grievance notice with regulatory citations and declaration.

6. **Step 6: Lifecycle Tracking & SCORES Escalation (Resolution)**
   - Click **"Save & Track"** or open **Tracker**.
   - View complaint `#INV-10234` in the animated timeline:
     - `Submitted` ✓ → `Acknowledged` ✓ → `Under Review` ●.
   - Inspect the countdown: **14 days remaining** under SEBI's mandatory 21-day timeline.
   - Test stage transitions or click **"Escalate to SCORES 2.0"**.

7. **Bonus: Adaptive Safety Academy (Prevention)**
   - Open **Safety Academy**.
   - Observe Safety Score progression: `68 → 76 → 82`.
   - Take the 4-question scenario dilemma quiz and see the score recalculate live!
