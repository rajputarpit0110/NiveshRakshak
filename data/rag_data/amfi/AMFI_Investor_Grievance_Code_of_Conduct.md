# Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI): Grievance & Intermediary Code

## Section 1: Intermediary Conduct & Fair Dealing
1.1 Mutual Fund Distributors (MFDs) and intermediaries registered with AMFI holding an ARN (AMFI Registration Number) shall:
- Adhere strictly to SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996.
- Refrain from making exaggerated claims, false projections, or promising guaranteed returns on market-linked schemes.
- Disclose all commissions (trail, transaction charges) received from Asset Management Companies (AMCs) to the investor.
- Never encourage churning or unnecessary switching between mutual fund schemes purely to generate commission revenue.

## Section 2: Delayed Redemption and Dividend Payouts
2.1 As per SEBI regulations, redemption proceeds must be dispatched or credited to the investor's verified bank account within **3 working days** of the redemption request date (T+3).
2.2 For Liquid and Overnight schemes, redemption proceeds must be credited within **T+1 working day**.
2.3 Penalty for Delay:
- If the AMC delays redemption beyond the prescribed timeframe, it is mandated to pay interest at **15% per annum** for the period of delay to the unit holder.

## Section 3: Redressal Hierarchy for Mutual Fund Grievances
3.1 Step 1: Investor lodges grievance with the Registrar and Transfer Agent (CAMS / KFintech) or directly with the AMC Investor Relations Officer (IRO). Turnaround time is **15 working days**.
3.2 Step 2: If unresolved, escalate to the AMC Compliance Officer.
3.3 Step 3: If still unresolved or unsatisfied after 21 days, file a complaint on the SEBI SCORES portal or AMFI Grievance Cell.
