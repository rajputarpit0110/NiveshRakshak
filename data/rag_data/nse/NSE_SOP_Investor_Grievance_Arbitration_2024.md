# National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) — SOP for Investor Grievances

## Section 1: Investor Services Cell (ISC) Framework
1.1 NSE maintains Investor Service Cells across Regional Arbitration Centres (RACs) to resolve monetary and service disputes between trading members and investors.
1.2 Grievance Registration:
- An investor may register a complaint through the NSE NICE Plus portal (niceplus.nseindia.com) or via email to ignse@nse.co.in.
- The complaint must state the Member Name, SEBI Registration Number, Unique Client Code (UCC), monetary claim value, and relevant trade dates.

## Section 2: Investor Grievance Redressal Committee (IGRC)
2.1 When a dispute cannot be resolved directly between the member and investor within 15 days, it is referred to the IGRC.
2.2 The IGRC consists of independent experts (retired judicial officers, senior advocates, financial professionals).
2.3 Timeline:
- The IGRC meeting shall be conducted within **21 calendar days** of referral.
- The IGRC order is issued within **30 days** of filing.
2.4 Financial Relief:
- If IGRC rules in favour of the client for a claim up to ₹20,00,000, 50% of the admissible claim amount or ₹20 Lakh (whichever is lower) may be released to the client from the Member's security deposit or Investor Protection Fund (IPF), pending arbitration.

## Section 3: Exchange Arbitration Mechanism
3.1 If either party is dissatisfied with the IGRC decision, they can file for Arbitration through the SMART ODR platform within **30 calendar days** of the IGRC order.
3.2 Arbitral Tribunal:
- Disputes up to ₹50 Lakh are adjudicated by a sole arbitrator.
- Disputes exceeding ₹50 Lakh are adjudicated by a panel of three arbitrators.
3.3 Arbitration awards must normally be pronounced within **4 months** of the arbitrator's appointment.
