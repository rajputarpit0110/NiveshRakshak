# Standard Escalation Matrix & Regulatory Timelines

## Step 1: Intermediary Level (First Line of Redressal)
- **Channel**: Stock Broker / Depository Participant Grievance Redressal Desk.
- **Mandatory Turnaround Time (TAT)**: Up to **21 calendar days**.
- **Investor Right**: Written acknowledgment within 48 hours; detailed Action Taken Report (ATR) explaining reasons for charge or debit within 21 days.
- **Escalation Trigger**: If unresolved after 21 days, or if the investor receives an unsatisfactory response.

## Step 2: Designated Body / Stock Exchange Level (Second Line of Redressal)
- **Channel**: NSE Investor Service Cell (ISC) / BSE Investor Services Cell.
- **Turnaround Time (TAT)**: Up to **30 calendar days** (IGRC order within 30 days).
- **Process**: Exchange seeks formal reply from member; if unresolved, dispute goes before the Investor Grievance Redressal Committee (IGRC).
- **Escalation Trigger**: Failure to resolve or dissatisfaction with IGRC decision within 30 calendar days.

## Step 3: Regulatory Level (SEBI SCORES 2.0)
- **Channel**: SEBI Complaint Redress System (scores.sebi.gov.in / SCORES Mobile App).
- **Mandatory TAT**: Entity must upload ATR within **21 calendar days** of complaint receipt on SCORES.
- **Review Period**: Investor has 15 days to indicate satisfaction or dissatisfaction with the ATR.

## Step 4: Dispute Adjudication (SMART ODR / Arbitration)
- **Channel**: Smart Online Dispute Resolution (smartodr.in).
- **Conciliation Stage**: Completed within **21 days**.
- **Arbitration Stage**: Arbitral award pronounced within **4 months**.
