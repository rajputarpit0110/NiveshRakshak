# SEBI Investor Charter: Stock Brokers & Depository Participants

## Section 1: Vision and Mission of Investor Protection
1.1 Vision: To protect the interests of investors in securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate the securities market.
1.2 Mission: To ensure that stock brokers provide services with transparency, fair dealing, and prompt redressal of grievances.

## Section 2: Fundamental Rights of Investors
Investors trading through registered stock brokers are entitled to the following non-negotiable rights:
2.1 **Right to Transparency in Charges**:
- Receive a complete tariff sheet / schedule of charges at the time of account opening.
- No broker shall levy any charge, fee, or penalty that was not explicitly disclosed in the agreed schedule of charges.
- Any change in tariff must be communicated to the client at least **30 days in advance**.
2.2 **Right to Contract Notes**:
- Receive digitally signed electronic contract notes (ECN) within **24 hours** of trade execution to the registered email address.
- Contract notes must clearly break down: trade price, brokerage, Securities Transaction Tax (STT), Exchange Turnover charges, GST, and SEBI turnover fees.
2.3 **Right to Quarterly / Monthly Settlement of Funds and Securities**:
- Running account settlement of funds must be executed by the broker on the first Friday of every quarter or month, as opted by the client.
- Unutilized funds must be refunded to the investor's bank account without any deduction or delay.
2.4 **Right to Segregation of Client Assets**:
- Stock brokers are strictly prohibited from mixing client funds or securities with proprietary funds.
- Client collateral cannot be pledged for broker proprietary obligations.
2.5 **Right to Receive Periodic Statements**:
- Receive daily margin statements, monthly holding statements, and quarterly ledger summaries.

## Section 3: Prohibited Activities by Stock Brokers
3.1 Stock brokers and their authorized persons are prohibited from:
- Promising guaranteed, assured, or fixed returns on stock market investments.
- Trading in a client's account without explicit instruction or Power of Attorney / DDPI mandate.
- Levying arbitrary administrative or handling charges without written authorization.
- Sharing client credentials (passwords, OTPs, PINs) or accessing client terminals remotely.

## Section 4: Responsibilities of Investors
4.1 Always verify that the stock broker is registered with SEBI by checking their Registration Number on the SEBI portal.
4.2 Never share OTPs, demat passwords, or trading credentials with anyone, including relationship managers.
4.3 Scrutinize SMS and email trade alerts received from stock exchanges (NSE/BSE) and compare them with the contract note.
4.4 Promptly flag unauthorized transactions or unrecognized debits within **24 to 48 hours** to the broker's compliance officer.
