# SEBI Master Circular: Investor Grievance Redressal Mechanism and SCORES 2.0

## Section 1: Overview and Applicability
1.1 This Master Circular consolidates the operating guidelines for handling investor grievances against listed companies and SEBI-registered intermediaries, including Stock Brokers, Depository Participants, Merchant Bankers, Portfolio Managers, and Mutual Funds.
1.2 The primary objective of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Complaint Redress System (SCORES 2.0) is to provide an efficient, transparent, and technology-driven mechanism for speedy redressal of investor grievances.

## Section 2: Two-Tier Grievance Redressal Process
2.1 **Tier 1 (Direct with Intermediary)**:
- Investors must first lodge their complaint directly with the concerned entity/intermediary through their designated grievance redressal portal or dedicated email ID.
- The intermediary is mandated to redress the grievance within a maximum of **21 calendar days** from the date of receipt.
- The intermediary must provide a reasoned Action Taken Report (ATR) explaining whether the grievance is resolved, partially resolved, or rejected with grounds.

2.2 **Tier 2 (SCORES 2.0 Platform)**:
- If the intermediary fails to respond within 21 days or if the investor is dissatisfied with the resolution provided, the investor may escalate the complaint to SEBI via the SCORES 2.0 portal (scores.sebi.gov.in).
- Upon registration on SCORES, the complaint is routed automatically to the designated entity.
- The entity must resolve and upload the ATR within **21 calendar days**.
- In case of non-resolution or inadequate ATR, the complaint is reviewed by the Designated Body (Stock Exchange or Depositories).

## Section 3: Escalation to Online Dispute Resolution (SMART ODR)
3.1 If the investor remains aggrieved after the review by the Designated Body, the complaint can be channeled to the SMART ODR Portal (Smart Online Dispute Resolution).
3.2 The SMART ODR mechanism comprises Conciliation followed by Arbitration:
- Conciliation proceedings shall be completed within **21 days**.
- If conciliation fails, the investor may opt for arbitration governed by stock exchange bye-laws.

## Section 4: Document Requirements for Lodging Grievances
4.1 The investor must furnish clear evidence including:
- Client Code / Unique Client Code (UCC) and Demat Account Number (DP ID + Client ID).
- Contract notes, ledger statements, or bank transaction statements reflecting the disputed debit.
- Copy of prior correspondence with the intermediary demonstrating initial grievance lodging.
- Detailed chronology of events.
