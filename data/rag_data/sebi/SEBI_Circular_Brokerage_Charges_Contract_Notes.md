# SEBI Circular: Disclosures on Brokerage, Levies, Penalties, and Ledger Debits

## Section 1: Prohibition of Undisclosed Debits
1.1 No stock broker, clearing member, or depository participant shall debit any amount from a client's trading ledger or demat account without an explicit, verifiable accounting justification.
1.2 Generic remarks in financial ledgers such as "Administrative Charges", "Internal Fine", "Penalty Adjustment", "Sundry Debit", or "Handling Fees" without itemized line-item invoices or supporting computation are strictly non-compliant under SEBI inspection norms.
1.3 In the event of any disputed deduction (e.g. ₹500, ₹2,500, or any amount), the broker must provide an itemized breakdown within **3 working days** upon investor inquiry.

## Section 2: Account Maintenance Charges (AMC) & Dormant Accounts
2.1 Basic Services Demat Account (BSDA) holders holding debt/equity securities with market value up to ₹4,00,000 are entitled to zero AMC or strictly capped statutory charges.
2.2 For regular trading and demat accounts:
- AMC and periodic maintenance fees must be published in advance in the standard Tariff Sheet signed during Client Registration Form (CRF) onboarding.
- Brokers cannot retroactively debit dormant accounts for inactive periods if no prior written intimation was served.

## Section 3: Margin Call Penalties and Peak Margin Debits
3.1 Short-margin penalty levied by Clearing Corporations may only be passed on to clients if the client genuinely failed to maintain requisite initial or peak margin.
3.2 The broker is obligated to furnish:
- The exchange margin snapshot timestamp.
- Client ledger balance at the time of trade.
- Exact shortage amount calculation.
If the broker fails to provide the exchange shortage report, the debit must be refunded to the client immediately.

## Section 4: Investor Remedy for Unauthorized Ledger Debits
4.1 The investor should immediately send a written protest to the broker's compliance department attaching:
- The ledger extract showing the disputed debit entry.
- The contract notes of the relevant settlement.
- The initial agreed schedule of charges.
4.2 If the broker fails to reverse the unexplained charge or provide satisfactory regulatory justification within **15 calendar days**, the investor is advised to lodge a complaint on the SEBI SCORES portal under the category *"Stock Broker / Unauthorized Charges / Disputed Financial Ledger Entry"*.
