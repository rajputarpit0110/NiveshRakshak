# Reserve Bank of India: Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021

## Section 1: Scope and Applicability
1.1 The Scheme applies to all Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks, Scheduled Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks, Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) meeting deposit or asset thresholds, and System Participants (Payment Gateways, Prepaid Payment Instruments).
1.2 The Scheme provides a cost-free and expeditious dispute resolution mechanism for deficiencies in financial service.

## Section 2: Grounds for Complaint
Investors and consumers may lodge complaints on grounds including:
2.1 **Unauthorized Electronic Fund Transfers**: Debits executed without client consent or dual authentication failure.
2.2 **Failure or Delay in Reversal of Failed Transactions**: Failure to reverse debited funds for failed ATM, UPI, IMPS, or POS transactions within prescribed turnaround times (T+1 to T+5 days) attracting statutory compensation of ₹100 per day of delay.
2.3 **Non-observance of Fair Practices Code**: Levying arbitrary processing charges or undisclosed foreclosure penalties.
2.4 **Recovery Agent Harassment**: Use of intimidation, abusive language, or violation of RBI recovery agent guidelines.

## Section 3: Escalation Hierarchy
3.1 Step 1: Complainant must first lodge a written complaint with the Regulated Entity (Bank / NBFC / Payment Gateway).
3.2 Step 2: If the Regulated Entity does not reply within **30 calendar days**, or rejects the complaint, or the complainant is not satisfied with the reply, a complaint can be filed with the RBI Ombudsman via the CMS portal (cms.rbi.org.in).
3.3 Award and Compensation:
- The Ombudsman can award compensation up to **₹30 Lakh** for actual monetary loss.
- In addition, compensation up to **₹1 Lakh** may be awarded for loss of time, expenses, and mental harassment.
