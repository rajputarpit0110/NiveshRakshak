# BSE Limited: Guidelines on Investor Protection and Unauthorized Trades

## Section 1: Pre-requisites for Legitimate Trade Execution
1.1 Trading members must maintain tangible records of client order placement:
- Telephone voice recordings through dedicated telecom lines with timestamping.
- Electronic logs of web/mobile trading applications showing IP address, device ID, and 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) verification.
- Written physical order slips with client signature for offline trade execution.
1.2 If a trading member executes trades on behalf of an investor without keeping the pre-trade consent record, such trades are deemed unauthorized and the financial loss is reversible to the investor.

## Section 2: Procedure upon Detecting Unauthorized Trading
2.1 An investor detecting unauthorized trading entries or unexplained debits must:
- Immediately intimate the member's compliance officer within 24 hours.
- Request the member to block further trading permissions on the account.
- File a formal dispute on the BSE Investor Redressal Portal (is.bseindia.com).
2.2 The member is obligated to produce the pre-trade order placement proof within **48 hours** to the exchange.
2.3 If the member fails to furnish proof of authorization, BSE shall initiate ledger reversal procedures and freeze disputed amounts from member settlement collateral.

## Section 3: Investor Protection Fund (IPF) Support
3.1 In cases where a trading member is declared a defaulter or expelled, genuine investor claims are eligible for compensation from the BSE Investor Protection Fund up to a maximum limit of **₹25,00,000 per investor**.
