# SEBI & NSE Public Advisory: Investment Risk & Scam Signal Indicators

## Section 1: Top 10 Red Flags in Investment Solicitations
Investors are cautioned against dealing with entities exhibiting any of the following high-risk indicators:
1. **Guaranteed / Assured Returns**: Any scheme promising fixed monthly returns (e.g. 10%, 20%, 30% per month) on equity, derivatives, or forex. Under SEBI regulations, no registered market participant is permitted to offer assured returns.
2. **Urgent Action / Pressure Tactics**: Messages demanding instant transfers ("Offer expires in 1 hour", "Only 3 slots remaining").
3. **Unverified Telegram / WhatsApp Groups**: Groups run by self-proclaimed "institutional traders", "foreign fund managers", or "SEBI-approved algorithm operators".
4. **Requests for Transfer to Personal Bank Accounts or Mule Accounts**: Legitimate brokers and AMCs only collect funds through approved payment gateways into designated client bank accounts (USCNB / client clearing accounts). Never transfer funds to personal savings accounts or third-party UPI handles.
5. **Requests to Install Remote Access Apps**: Software such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk, or QuickSupport requested under the pretext of "KYC verification" or "trading desk setup".
6. **Fake App Downloads via APKs**: Trading apps distributed via external links or Telegram rather than official Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
7. **Demand for Additional "Tax" or "Release Fees" to Withdraw Funds**: Fraudulent platforms show fake portfolio profits and demand 20-30% upfront "clearance fee" or "GST" before releasing profits. Genuine brokers debit taxes at source and never ask for advance release deposits.
8. **Impersonation of Regulated Entities or Dignitaries**: Using forged SEBI registration certificates, fake logos of NSE, BSE, or renowned institutional asset managers.
9. **Fake Pre-IPO and Exclusive Institutional Allotment Schemes**: Soliciting funds for grey-market pre-IPO shares with guaranteed multi-bagger listing gains.
10. **Requests for OTP, Demat MPIN, or Password Sharing**: Regulated intermediaries will never ask for your 2FA OTP, demat trading password, or biometric pin.

## Section 2: Verification Protocol for Investors
2.1 Always check the registration status on SEBI's official website (sebi.gov.in) under "Recognised Intermediaries".
2.2 Cross-check broker details on the official NSE Member Directory (nseindia.com) or BSE Member Directory (bseindia.com).
2.3 If a suspected entity claims SEBI registration, verify the exact Registration Number (e.g. INZ000... or INH000...) against the SEBI database.

## Section 3: Recommended Immediate Actions upon Suspecting a Scam
3.1 **Halt Further Transactions**: Do not transfer additional funds under any pretext, especially withdrawal unlock demands.
3.2 **Preserve Evidence**: Take screenshots of chat histories, transaction records, UPI UTR numbers, account details, and URLs.
3.3 **Report to National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal**: Call toll-free helpline **1930** or lodge a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in within golden hours (first 2-4 hours).
3.4 **Inform Bank & Freeze Accounts**: Immediately notify the remitting bank's fraud monitoring cell to initiate recall of transferred funds.
